# Chat first Today

Date: 2026-10-15
Reference: `ciatta-visual-assets/33d55fd10e37864168d0390429768cd9 (1).jpg`

## What changed

- **Ground.** Every screen's flat ground is lit the way the cards are: the
  screen's first hue falling softly from the top into the dark, its second
  pooled to one side, and the same grain. The light ends before the middle
  of the screen.
- **Today** (the reference's first screen):
  - The greeting is small (Subhead, regular) with a small calendar.
  - Pattern Insights is set as "What are we building today?": a large
    line, then a row of small glow cards, one per pattern, opening its
    page.
  - The chat card rests at the foot, at half the height the insights sheet
    had (22% of the screen), with "Ask Ciatta", attach and the microphone.
  - The menu button sits just above it.
  - The + button and the "Ask Ciatta anything" box are gone.
- **The chat** (the reference's other two screens):
  - Tapping the chat card opens the chat over the whole screen. The corner
    menu hides.
  - "Close chat" at the top brings her back to Today.
  - There is no avatar, no name, no greeting bubble, no suggestion chips
    and no action cards. Capabilities and interventions are still
    computed, and answer when asked.
  - A waiting check in is asked as Ciatta's first message, in words. Her
    reply is read for her timeline as any message is.

## Kept

  confirmation, not suggestions.
